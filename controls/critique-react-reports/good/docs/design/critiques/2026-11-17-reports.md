## Findings

### F1 [S2] `color.contrast`: The order rows are pale grey on white
- **Screenshot:** [default, 390](2026-11-17-reports/default-390.png) (state `default`, 390 wide)
- **Observed:** The five order rows below the filter show their numbers and totals ('#1001', '$19.99') in light grey on white cards, much fainter than the 'Reports' heading.
- **Fix:** Use `text-text` for the order rows and `text-muted` for the intro line.

### F2 [S3] `color.palette`: The filter button is bright magenta
- **Screenshot:** [default, 1280](2026-11-17-reports/default-1280.png) (state `default`, 1280 wide)
- **Observed:** The square filter button right of the search field is bright magenta, a colour used nowhere else on this screen or the Orders screen.
- **Fix:** Make it a `Button` with `variant="outline"` and `size="sm"`.
